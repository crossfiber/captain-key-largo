Straight answers

What your trip costs, and why Brian quotes it straight.

No inflated rack rates, no coupon games. Brian quotes each trip directly because a two-hour sunset barhop and a dusk to dawn expedition are not the same product.

How pricing worksQuoted per trip, same day
What moves the numberHours, trip type, season

Charter pricing is mostly arithmetic: hours on the water, fuel burned, and the season's demand. A one-boat operation with no booking-platform fees also skips the 15 to 20 percent that middleman sites quietly add, which is why booking direct with the captain is consistently the better number.

Every quote covers Brian as your USCG 100 Ton Master captain, the 2021 Sea Hunt 25 bay boat, and the local knowledge that is the actual product. Fishing trips include the gear and bait handled by the captain; specialty runs like lobster nights include the nets, lights, and legal know-how.

Pricing questions

Why not just post prices?

Because Brian builds trips to order: a 3 hour family snapper run, an 8 hour two-sided island day, and a dusk to dawn expedition deserve different numbers, not one padded rate.

Is booking direct really cheaper?

Booking platforms take a commission on every trip, which pads the price. Direct means the number funds the boat and the captain, not a website.

Do you require a deposit?

Booking terms are confirmed when Brian quotes your trip, before you commit. Ask him anything; you will be texting with the captain, not a call center.
