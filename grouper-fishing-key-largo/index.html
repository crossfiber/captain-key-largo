Captain Key LargoGrouper

Grouper live deeper than this boat goes.

Brian fishes the backcountry, the flats and the patch reefs out of Key Largo. Keeper-size gag and black grouper sit on deep structure further out, on a boat built to hold over it and winch fish up out of it.

Where they areDeep structure and wrecks
SeasonFWC windows, cooler months
The right boatHatteras with the tackle for it
Who runs itThe Poppa Wahoo Collective

Grouper fishing is a tackle problem before it is a fish problem. A grouper's whole strategy is to bury itself in structure the second it feels pressure, so you need heavy gear, a boat that can hold position over a wreck, and enough freeboard to work it. None of that describes a 25 foot bay boat, and Brian will tell you that rather than take the deposit.

What you can catch on the bottom here

Key Largo's patch reefs hold mangrove snapper year round and they are excellent eating, caught on light tackle in water you can see the bottom of. That is a real trip and Brian runs it constantly. It is just not a grouper trip.

Who in the family runs grouper

The Poppa Wahoo Collective, the Epsteins' Islamorada fleet, fishes gag grouper and mutton snapper on deep structure out of Three Waters Resort Marina, twenty minutes south on US-1. Their grouper page is here. Seasons are set by FWC and the captain will tell you what is open on your dates.

Same family, different water

Captain Key Largo is part of the collective. Brian is not sending you to a competitor, he is sending you to his own family's boat, because that is the one that can actually do the job.

Straight answers

Can we keep grouper?

That depends on FWC season windows and size limits, which move. Whichever boat you fish, the captain tells you what is legal on the day.

Does Brian fish for grouper at all?

Not the deep structure fishing. He runs patch reefs for mangrove snapper, which is a different and very good trip.

Who runs the deep bottom trips?

The family's Islamorada fleet, two Hatteras sportfishers out of Three Waters Resort Marina.

Is it the same business?

Same family. Captain Key Largo is part of The Poppa Wahoo Collective.
